Oil & Gas Pipeline

Onshore pipeline construction from right-of-way to commissioning.

Overview

Complete mainline construction

We build onshore oil and gas pipelines, including large-diameter transmission lines, using our own crews and equipment spreads.

  • Right-of-way clearing, grading and access roads
  • Pipe hauling, stringing and cold bending
  • Mechanized and manual welding and NDT
  • Weld repairs, field-joint coating and holiday testing
  • Trenching, lowering-in and backfill
  • Road, river and wetland crossings
  • Hydrostatic testing, drying and reinstatement
